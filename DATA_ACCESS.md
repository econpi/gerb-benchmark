# Accessing the GERB problem set

The sixty problem statements and their reference solutions are the private component of GERB. They
are withheld from public release so the benchmark stays out of training corpora and remains a valid
test of reasoning rather than recall. They are made available for research use under the agreement
below.

## Who may request access

Researchers evaluating language models, for non-commercial research use. Access is granted to a
named individual, not to an organization at large.

## What you agree to

By requesting and receiving the GERB problem set, you agree that you will:

1. **Not republish** the problems or reference solutions, in whole or in part, in any public
   venue, repository, website, or dataset.
2. **Not train on** the problems or solutions, and not include them in any corpus used to train,
   fine-tune, or otherwise adapt a model.
3. **Not incorporate** the problems into any product, service, or public leaderboard that would
   expose them.
4. Use the materials solely to evaluate models for research, and store them so they are not
   exposed to web crawlers or to third parties who have not signed this agreement.
5. Cite GERB in any resulting work, using the entries in [CITATION.bib](CITATION.bib).

These terms bind you and anyone you share the materials with. Sharing is permitted only with
people who have themselves agreed to these terms.

## How to request

Send an email to the address below with:

- your name and research affiliation,
- a one-paragraph description of the intended use,
- a statement that you accept the terms above.

**Contact:** pgade@albany.edu

Access is typically granted by return of a password-protected archive or an invitation to a gated
repository. There is no fee.

## Note for reviewers and program committees

The design, the grading rules, the grade ruling and the full results are public in this benchmark repository, so the method is fully reproducible without the private problems. The paper's full replication materials, the data and the analysis code, travel with the paper, not with this benchmark. The grade-ruling code, which encodes the verified answers of the tasks it regrades, is provided under the same terms as the problems. The problems can be provided to a program
committee or reviewer under the same terms on request.
